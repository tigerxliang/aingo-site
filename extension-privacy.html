PodPop Browser Extension Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

The PodPop browser extension replaces the subtitles of videos you watch with subtitles that translate only the words you may not know.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension has no account system and no servers of its own. We do not collect, sell or share personal information, and there is no analytics or advertising.

What the extension reads

On pages that play video, the extension reads the subtitle text of that video so it can show it again with translations. It does this on your device. Subtitle text is never sent to us. It does not read your browsing history, passwords, forms or anything else on the page.

Data stored on your device

Your language settings, subtitle style and the words you mark are stored in your browser. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

Network requests

The extension downloads its dictionary files from our public file host, and subtitle files from the video site that provides them. These are ordinary file downloads and contain none of your data.

Why it needs access to all sites

Videos can appear on any website, including players embedded in other sites, so the extension needs to run on pages where a video plays.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

PodPop 浏览器插件隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 5 日

PodPop 浏览器插件把你观看的视频字幕换成只翻译生词的字幕。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。插件没有账号系统，也没有自己的服务器。我们不收集、出售或分享个人信息，没有统计分析，也没有广告。

插件读取什么

在播放视频的页面上，插件读取这个视频的字幕文字，用来重新显示并加上释义。这一切都在你的设备上完成，字幕文字不会发给我们。插件不读取浏览记录、密码、表单或页面上的其他内容。

保存在你设备上的数据

语言设置、字幕样式和你标记的单词保存在浏览器里，卸载插件后会被删除。

网络请求

插件从我们的公开文件地址下载词典文件，从提供字幕的视频网站下载字幕文件。这些都是普通的文件下载，不包含你的数据。

为什么需要访问所有网站

视频可能出现在任何网站上，包括嵌在其他网站里的播放器，所以插件需要在播放视频的页面上运行。

变更

如果本政策有变更，我们会更新此页面和上面的生效日期。
