AINGO Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

AINGO is a language-learning app that plays public podcasts (Spanish, English and Chinese) with subtitles and marks words you may not know. We built it so that your learning data stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. AINGO has no account system and no servers of its own. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK in the app.

Data stored on your device

The following stays only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app: your subscriptions, your native and learning language settings, the words you mark or remove, your listening statistics, and the optional nickname, avatar, phone number and email you enter in your profile. This information is never uploaded.

Content from third parties

Podcast feeds, audio, subtitles and cover images are loaded directly from each podcast publisher's own public servers, the same way any podcast app works. Those publishers may see standard request information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. AINGO does not control that content.

Permissions

Photo library — only if you choose a profile picture. The selected image is kept on your device.
Background audio — so episodes keep playing when the screen is locked.

Children

AINGO does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

AINGO 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日

AINGO 是一款语言学习应用：播放公开的西班牙语、英语、中文播客和字幕，并标出你可能不认识的单词。我们的设计原则是：你的学习数据只留在你的手机上。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。AINGO 没有账号系统，也没有自己的服务器。我们不收集、存储、出售或分享任何个人信息，应用内没有任何统计、广告或追踪工具。

保存在你设备上的数据

以下内容只保存在你的设备上，删除应用即全部删除：订阅、母语和学习语言设置、标记或移除的单词、学习统计，以及你在个人资料里自愿填写的昵称、头像、手机号和邮箱。这些信息不会被上传。

来自第三方的内容

播客节目列表、音频、字幕和封面图直接从各播客发布方自己的公开服务器加载，和所有播客应用一样。发布方可能看到 IP 地址等常规请求信息，适用其各自的隐私政策。AINGO 不控制这些内容。

权限

相册：仅在你设置头像时使用，所选图片只保存在本机。
后台音频：锁屏后继续播放。
