src/data/examples.json contains example sentences from Tatoeba
(https://tatoeba.org), licensed under CC-BY 2.0 FR
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/).
Traditional Chinese translations were converted to Simplified Chinese.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
Recommended channels (src/data/recommended.js) are public podcast RSS
feeds. Audio and transcripts are streamed directly from each
publisher's own URLs and remain the property of their publishers.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
src/data/en/gloss.{ja,ko,es,pt}.json (English → Japanese / Korean / Spanish / Portuguese)
are built by scripts/build-gloss-intl.py from:
- Wiktionary (English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish and Portuguese editions), via kaikki.org,
  licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- Open Multilingual Wordnet (https://omwn.org): Japanese WordNet (NICT, WordNet-style license),
  Multilingual Central Repository for Spanish (CC BY 3.0), OpenWN-PT for Portuguese (CC BY-SA 3.0),
  mapped through Princeton WordNet 3.0 (WordNet License)
- EJDict-hand (https://github.com/kujirahand/EJDict), public domain
- kengdic (https://github.com/garfieldnate/kengdic), MPL 2.0

Example sentence translations into Japanese, Korean, Spanish and Portuguese are from Tatoeba
(https://tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR.
- Basic Korean Dictionary (한국어기초사전, National Institute of Korean Language,
  https://krdict.korean.go.kr), CC BY-SA 2.0 KR (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/kr/)
- Remaining common words: translations written for this app (scripts/gloss-extra/)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
src/data/fr/ (French) is built by scripts/build-dict-fr.py from FrequencyWords fr_50k
(OpenSubtitles, CC BY-SA 4.0), English and Chinese Wiktionary (kaikki.org, CC BY-SA 4.0)
and ECDICT (MIT); example sentences from Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR).

src/data/ko/ (Korean) is built by scripts/build-dict-ko.py from the Basic Korean Dictionary
(한국어기초사전, National Institute of Korean Language, CC BY-SA 2.0 KR: headwords, English equivalents,
pronunciation, hanja, levels), FrequencyWords ko_full (OpenSubtitles, CC BY-SA 4.0), Chinese Wiktionary
Korean entries via kaikki.org (CC BY-SA) and ECDICT (MIT); word frequencies are lemmatised with the Kiwi
morphological analyzer (kiwipiepy, LGPL, build time only); example sentences from Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR).

src/data/ja/ (Japanese) is built by scripts/build-dict-ja.py from JMdict (Electronic Dictionary Research
and Development Group, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html: English glosses, readings),
Tatoeba Japanese sentences (CC BY 2.0 FR: word frequencies, word forms, example sentences) segmented with
MeCab/UniDic (fugashi + unidic-lite, build time only), Chinese Wiktionary Japanese entries via kaikki.org
(CC BY-SA) and ECDICT (MIT).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
src/data/zh/gloss.{ja,ko}.json, src/data/ja/gloss.ko.json and src/data/ko/gloss.ja.json
(Chinese → Japanese / Korean, Japanese → Korean, Korean → Japanese) are built by
scripts/build-gloss-cjk.py from Japanese and Korean Wiktionary (via kaikki.org, CC BY-SA 4.0),
bridged through this app's existing Chinese glosses; common function words written for this app.
Japanese and Korean translations of the Chinese / Japanese / Korean example sentences are from
Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
src/data/de/ (German), src/data/it/ (Italian) and src/data/pt/ (Portuguese) are built by
scripts/build-dict-latin.py from FrequencyWords de_50k / it_50k / pt_50k (OpenSubtitles, CC BY-SA 4.0),
English and Chinese Wiktionary (kaikki.org, CC BY-SA 4.0) and ECDICT (MIT);
example sentences from Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR), Chinese translations partly via the English translation.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
src/data/{ja,ko}/gloss.{vi,id,th,es,pt}.json and src/data/zh/gloss.{vi,id,th,es,ru}.json are built by
scripts/build-gloss-pivot.py from Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian Wiktionary
(via kaikki.org, CC BY-SA 4.0), English Wiktionary translation tables, and this app's English dictionaries;
the most common words are written for this app (scripts/gloss-extra/pivot-common.tsv).
Example sentence translations are from Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR), partly via the English translation.
Between European languages (src/data/{es,pt,fr,de,it}/gloss.{es,pt,fr,de,ru,ar}.json) the same script also uses
Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Russian Wiktionary (kaikki.org, CC BY-SA 4.0);
common words written for this app (scripts/gloss-extra/pivot-common-eu.tsv).
